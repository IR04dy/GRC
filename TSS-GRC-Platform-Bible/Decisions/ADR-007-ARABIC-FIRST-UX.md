# ADR-007 — Arabic-First UX

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-25

## Decision

Arabic is designed at component, terminology, content and workflow level. Material records require both language values unless a documented exception applies. Directionality is component-aware; identifiers, dates and numbers remain readable in mixed-direction content.

## Alternatives

- translation after implementation: rejected;
- Arabic-only product: rejected because enterprise and external assurance commonly require English;
- automatic machine translation as authoritative content: rejected.

## Consequences

Higher content governance and QA cost; substantial Saudi usability and sales advantage.

## Proof

Arabic persona tests, RTL visual regression, bilingual validation, search and export parity.
