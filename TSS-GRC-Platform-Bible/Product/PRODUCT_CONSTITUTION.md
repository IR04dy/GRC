# Product Constitution

## Purpose

TSS GRC exists to help Saudi organisations make defensible decisions, reduce duplicated assurance work and demonstrate control effectiveness.

## Non-negotiable articles

### Article 1 — Evidence before assertion

The product may state what evidence supports. It must distinguish declared, tested, observed, approved and independently assured status.

### Article 2 — Arabic is a first-class product language

Arabic is not a translated skin. Arabic labels, content, validation, search, export, dates, numbers, legal terms, help and decision records must be designed and tested. English remains equally operable.

### Article 3 — Authority is server-derived

Tenant, actor, role, membership, authority, timestamps and approval eligibility are derived from authenticated and effective records, never trusted from request bodies.

### Article 4 — Four eyes for consequential decisions

No user may request and approve the same high-risk action. Exceptions are explicit, time-bound, separately authorised and auditable.

### Article 5 — One object, one owner

Every risk, control, obligation, evidence item, issue, party, asset and decision has one authoritative bounded context. Other modules reference it through published contracts, not shared repositories.

### Article 6 — Packages are real

An independently sold module has:

- a stable package code and entitlement;
- declared kernel and optional dependencies;
- its own navigation and permission bundle;
- owned data and APIs;
- install, upgrade, disable, export and removal behaviour;
- isolation and contract tests.

If any condition is absent, it is a feature area, not an independently sellable module.

### Article 7 — Work is organised around outcomes

The primary UX unit is a user task: assess, approve, investigate, respond, certify or report. Database tables and backend package names do not define navigation.

### Article 8 — Configuration over forks

Sector, regulator, workflow, scoring, terminology and branding variation use versioned configuration and content packs. Customer-specific code forks are prohibited.

### Article 9 — Secure and private by design

Deny by default, minimum data, explicit purpose, classification, retention, audit and abuse cases are design inputs.

### Article 10 — AI advises; accountable humans decide

AI outputs include source, confidence, rationale and limitations. Material decisions require human confirmation. The product records acceptance, modification and rejection.

### Article 11 — Saudi-first, internationally credible

Local regulatory depth is maintained alongside ISO, COSO, IIA, IFRS and recognised security/privacy practices. Local relevance must not become hard-coded fragility.

### Article 12 — No irreversible architecture fashion

The default is a well-enforced modular monolith. Services are extracted only for proven scale, security, residency, team autonomy or release-cadence reasons.

## Product success measures

- time from framework activation to first evidence-backed posture;
- percentage of common controls reused across obligations;
- overdue assurance work and issue ageing;
- decision-cycle time without weakened SoD;
- Arabic task success and accessibility success;
- package attach rate and deployment lead time;
- evidence accepted without rework by audit or regulator;
- renewal, module expansion and referenceability.
