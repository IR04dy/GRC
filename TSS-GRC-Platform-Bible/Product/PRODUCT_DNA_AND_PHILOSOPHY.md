# Product DNA and Philosophy

## Product DNA

1. **Arabic-native:** language, culture and Saudi governance practices shape the interaction model.
2. **Evidence-centred:** evidence is an active, versioned object linked to claims and decisions.
3. **Connected, not entangled:** modules share meaning through contracts while retaining ownership.
4. **Opinionated defaults, configurable execution:** rapid implementation without customer forks.
5. **Board-readable, practitioner-deep:** executive truth is traceable to operational facts.
6. **Human-accountable AI:** automation accelerates judgement without obscuring responsibility.
7. **Composable commercial model:** start with one outcome and expand without reimplementation.

## Product philosophy

### Reduce compliance theatre

A percentage dashboard without scope, freshness, evidence and exceptions is decoration. Every posture number must be explainable.

### Design for the second line without punishing the first

Business owners should receive concise tasks with context, examples and deadlines. GRC specialists retain advanced configuration and analytics.

### Progressive disclosure

Default views answer “what needs my attention?” Advanced scoring, mappings and history remain one level deeper.

### Preserve dissent

Risk acceptance, committee voting, audit responses and AI recommendations must retain disagreement, conditions and rationale.

### Make assurance reusable

One control test and one approved evidence item may support several obligations, but reuse must expose scope and freshness.

## Design principles

- show ownership, due date, status and next action on every work object;
- use stable human-readable references, not database IDs;
- present Arabic first for Arabic tenants while retaining the English source and translation state;
- never make colour the only status signal;
- prefer a guided decision page to a large modal;
- make destructive or regulated actions explain consequences before confirmation;
- let users save drafts and resume;
- expose why a field is required and which obligation consumes it;
- show lineage from executive metric to underlying records;
- design empty states as onboarding steps, not dead ends.
