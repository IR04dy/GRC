# Future Vision

## 2028 product vision

TSS GRC becomes the Saudi assurance operating system: organisations activate the packages and regulatory content they need, connect operational systems, and receive an Arabic-first, evidence-backed view of risk and compliance.

## Experience

- every persona begins with outcome-based My Work;
- executives receive decision briefs, not dashboards full of percentages;
- first-line owners complete concise tasks in familiar channels;
- external parties use accessible purpose-built portals;
- Arabic and English records remain governed throughout their lifecycle;
- users can ask “why is this status red?” and receive traceable evidence, not generated guesswork.

## Architecture

- stable kernel and independently versioned packages;
- published contracts and event-backed projections;
- customer-selectable deployment/isolation tiers;
- certified connectors and partner extensions;
- evidence graph and semantic risk/control vocabulary;
- selective services for proven high-volume or isolation needs.

## Intelligence

- regulatory change is triaged against active content and customer context;
- control/evidence reuse recommendations reduce assurance effort;
- risk and control anomalies focus human review;
- agents prepare reversible work under policy;
- humans remain accountable for material decisions.

## Saudi leadership

- fastest activation of Saudi regulatory workflows;
- trusted Arabic terminology and content updates;
- government-grade accessibility and deployment;
- local implementation partner ecosystem;
- benchmark insights for Saudi sectors using privacy-preserving aggregation.

## Business model

Customers enter through one urgent outcome, then expand packages on the same kernel. Content, connectors, managed assurance and AI create recurring value beyond licence count. Partners build certified extensions without forking the product.

## North-star measures

- first evidence-backed posture within 30 days;
- 50% reduction in duplicate evidence requests;
- 30% reduction in approval cycle time without SoD exceptions;
- more than 90% on-time high-risk action closure;
- Arabic task success equal to or better than English;
- package deployment without regression to installed modules;
- expanding annual recurring revenue from package attach and content services.
