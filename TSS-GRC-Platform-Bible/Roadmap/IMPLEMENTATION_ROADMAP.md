# Implementation Roadmap

## Governing sequence

**Assure → simplify → package → prove → scale.**

No roadmap credit is earned for adding screens while P0 decision integrity remains open.

## Phase 0 — Truth and containment (0–30 days)

### Outcomes

- critical change/CAB and committee defects closed;
- false completion claims corrected;
- package map and architecture principles approved;
- universal security/entitlement work designed;
- UX and Saudi research scheduled.

### Deliverables

1. authenticated actor binding for change approvals and committee votes;
2. named change decision commands with `APPROVE`;
3. membership-derived attendance/quorum;
4. `EXPORT` authorization;
5. bilingual DTO validation for material change/decision records;
6. committee and change adversarial test packs;
7. architecture dependency baseline and ratchet;
8. story/document truth reconciliation;
9. approve ADR-001 through ADR-008;
10. appoint Product Council, Architecture Council and Saudi Content Office.

### Exit gate

No Critical finding open; two-principal live probes retained; 2,316+ tests remain green; corrected claims published.

## Phase 1 — Platform kernel and flagship UX (31–90 days)

### Outcomes

- backend package entitlement becomes universal;
- route/navigation manifests introduced;
- one decision/authority contract defined;
- My Work and guided decision experience piloted;
- first lighthouse package boundary selected.

### Deliverables

- package registry and manifests;
- entitlement interceptor covering controller, export, job and integration;
- kernel dependency rule;
- canonical object ownership decisions;
- frontend package route registry;
- My Work queue and role shell;
- guided decision component;
- evidence graph MVP;
- WCAG component baseline;
- official content pack metadata model;
- production-like migration/restore CI.

### Exit gate

Kernel boots without commercial packages. Unlicensed API tests pass for three migrated packages. Lighthouse journey passes Arabic/English moderated usability.

## Phase 2 — Composable product proof (3–6 months)

### Outcomes

- three sellable packages reach independence L3;
- five flagship journeys redesigned;
- Saudi content lifecycle operational;
- operational acceptance evidence assembled.

### Candidate package order

1. ERM;
2. Privacy;
3. Compliance;
4. BCM;
5. TPRM/SAMA Resilience.

Final order follows customer pipeline and dependency evidence.

### Deliverables

- owner contracts and read projections;
- per-package permissions, migrations and disable policy;
- Arabic search and bilingual export standard;
- independent WCAG 2.2 AA audit;
- tenant/security penetration test;
- performance, backup/restore and upgrade rehearsal;
- package-level sales demos and onboarding templates;
- three lighthouse customer pilots.

### Exit gate

At least three packages install/enable/disable independently; no forbidden imports added; government pilot gates satisfied.

## Phase 3 — Saudi enterprise general availability (6–12 months)

### Outcomes

- eight core packages at L3/L4;
- Saudi deployment tiers documented and priced;
- connected assurance/evidence graph established;
- sales and partner enablement ready.

### Deliverables

- ERM, Compliance, Policy, Audit, Privacy, BCM, TPRM and Governance packages;
- NCA/SAMA/PDPL/DGA content packs with update SLA;
- customer-admin package marketplace;
- connector certification framework;
- warehouse/analytics feed;
- AI gateway and first three evaluated copilots;
- reference architectures for government, financial and critical infrastructure;
- customer success value dashboard.

### Exit gate

Independent security/accessibility assurance, witnessed DR, three referenceable customers, renewal/expansion evidence and no unmitigated Critical/High acceptance defect.

## Phase 4 — Ecosystem and selective services (12–24 months)

- partner SDK and certified extensions;
- content marketplace;
- advanced continuous-control integrations;
- multi-region Saudi SaaS operations if commercially justified;
- extract high-volume ingestion, notifications or AI gateway only on evidence;
- bounded task agents;
- Arabic GRC knowledge and benchmark network using anonymised/consented data.

## Workstreams

| Workstream | Accountable leader | Core metric |
|---|---|---|
| Product and packaging | CPO | package adoption and time to value |
| Architecture | Chief Architect | forbidden dependencies and package level |
| Security | CISO/Product Security | critical abuse cases passing |
| UX | Head of Product Design | journey success and Arabic parity |
| Saudi content | Regulatory Content Lead | update SLA and mapping review |
| Data/integration | Data/Integration Architect | canonical ownership and contract health |
| Quality/operations | QA/Platform Ops | acceptance evidence and SLO |
| Sales/customer success | CRO/CS lead | proof-to-contract and expansion |

## Delivery capacity

Minimum sustained transformation team:

- 1 product director and 3 domain product managers;
- 1 chief/enterprise architect and 2 senior application architects;
- 2 product designers plus Arabic content designer/researcher;
- 2 security engineers;
- 3–4 cross-functional engineering squads;
- 2 QA automation/SDET;
- Saudi regulatory SMEs;
- DevOps/SRE and data/integration engineering;
- customer implementation and enablement lead.

Attempting this roadmap as documentation work beside normal feature delivery will fail.

## Programme controls

- fortnightly evidence-based outcome review;
- monthly architecture and product councils;
- no new package without PDS and manifest;
- 20% capacity reserved for structural debt until L3 baseline;
- quarterly customer advisory board;
- release gate based on journeys and controls, not story count.
