# Saudi Market and Regulatory Readiness

## Opinion

Saudi depth is TSS GRC's strongest strategic differentiator. The platform already contains meaningful workflows for NCA, SAMA, PDPL, NDI, BCM, Sharia, open banking and Saudi sustainability. The weakness is governance of regulatory truth: framework names and seeded content must be versioned, officially sourced, legally reviewed and updateable without code release.

## Regulatory operating model

Each content pack has:

- issuing authority and official source URL;
- instrument name, version, publication/effective dates;
- applicability rules;
- Arabic authoritative text and reviewed English where available;
- obligation decomposition;
- control mappings with reviewer/confidence;
- change history;
- customer activation and supersession;
- impact workflow and release notes;
- legal/SME approver.

The product never claims that mapping equals compliance.

## Saudi UX acceptance

Government and large-enterprise readiness requires:

- Arabic-first end-to-end journeys, not translated labels;
- WCAG 2.2 AA and Saudi DGA accessibility evidence;
- mobile-capable public and crisis flows;
- dual Gregorian/Hijri display where customer policy requires;
- Saudi work calendar and configurable official holidays;
- formal delegations and committee authority;
- Saudi-region hosting option and transparent data location;
- bilingual regulator/board output;
- local support, implementation and regulatory-content update operating model.

## Saudi deployment profiles

### Government profile

Arabic-first, DGA-aligned accessibility, NCA controls, government identity/integration constraints, Saudi hosting, formal acceptance evidence.

### Financial institution profile

SAMA control, outsourcing/cloud, operational resilience, IAM and audit packs; higher isolation, privileged access and DR evidence.

### Critical infrastructure profile

NCA plus critical/OT controls as applicable, on-prem/private cloud option, offline continuity, SIEM/OT integrations and strict evidence handling.

### Enterprise profile

Composable ERM/compliance/privacy/TPRM with Saudi content and lower implementation complexity.

## Regulatory-content team

Create a standing Saudi Content Office:

- regulatory lead;
- Arabic legal editor;
- NCA/cyber SME;
- SAMA financial-services SME;
- privacy/DPO SME;
- data/DGA/NDMO SME;
- product analyst and QA.

Service-level targets cover monitoring, triage, impact assessment, content release and urgent customer notice.
