# Persona and Customer Journeys

## Persona set

| Persona | Primary question | UX promise |
|---|---|---|
| CEO / Board member | Where are we exposed and what needs my decision? | Five-minute defensible view |
| CRO / Risk manager | Are risks within appetite and being treated? | One connected risk workspace |
| Compliance officer | Which obligations are unmet or changing? | Obligation-to-evidence traceability |
| Internal auditor | What should we audit and can findings be defended? | Risk-based, independent audit lifecycle |
| CISO / Cyber GRC | Are key controls effective against current threats? | Continuous control and issue context |
| DPO | Can we prove lawful, minimal and timely processing? | PDPL case and processing accountability |
| BCM manager | Can critical services recover within tolerance? | BIA-to-exercise evidence |
| Vendor-risk manager | Which third parties threaten critical services? | Lifecycle and concentration view |
| Control owner | What exactly must I do? | Minimal, contextual tasks |
| Committee secretary | Is the meeting decision valid and complete? | Agenda-to-signed-resolution flow |
| Platform administrator | Is the tenant safe, licensed and healthy? | Governed configuration and operations |
| External vendor/data subject | Can I complete my request safely? | Clear, accessible, status-aware portal |

## CEO journey

**Trigger:** quarterly review or material event.

1. Open Executive Home; see changes since last visit.
2. Review three categories: outside appetite, overdue critical remediation and decisions required.
3. Open one risk; see business impact, trend, accountable executive and evidence freshness.
4. Compare management proposal and alternatives.
5. Approve, reject or conditionally accept with expiry and rationale.
6. Generate bilingual board pack whose metrics drill to source.

**Redesign requirement:** remove operational tables and framework percentages from the first screen. Use business services, strategic objectives and financial/operational impact.

**Success:** material decision in under ten minutes; no unexplained metric; no access to inappropriate sensitive case details.

## Risk-manager journey

1. Receive KRI breach or risk-identification intake.
2. Validate taxonomy, affected objective/service and owner.
3. Assess inherent risk using configured method.
4. Link controls and review latest effectiveness.
5. Model treatments and residual risk.
6. Route acceptance to correct authority based on appetite and delegation.
7. Monitor treatment, exceptions and emerging indicators.

**Current risk:** risk functionality is deep but distributed across register, KRI, FAIR, bow-tie, scenarios and lifecycle pages.

**Target:** one risk workspace with progressive tools and one authoritative rating timeline.

## Auditor journey

1. Build auditable universe from risks, entities, processes and prior findings.
2. Propose risk-based annual plan.
3. Obtain Audit Committee approval with authenticated members.
4. Scope engagement and independence declaration.
5. Execute procedures with signed working papers.
6. Raise finding, obtain management response and preserve disagreement.
7. Follow CAPA without surrendering audit independence.
8. Report to committee with lineage.

**Acceptance:** plan approval and finding disposition cannot be authored by the auditor under a different name; evidence versions remain immutable.

## Compliance-officer journey

1. Activate regulator/framework content version.
2. Triage new or changed obligations.
3. Map to common controls with confidence and reviewer.
4. Assign owners and evidence cadence.
5. Run assessment/attestation.
6. Create issue and CAPA for deficiency.
7. Produce regulator or management view with exceptions and scope.

**Target:** regulatory horizon is integrated with obligations; the officer does not re-enter controls per framework.

## Customer acquisition and onboarding journey

1. Discovery captures sector, regulators, deployment constraints, personas, current systems and top five outcomes.
2. Solution configuration demonstrates only relevant packages.
3. Proof of value uses customer-like data and measurable cycle-time reduction.
4. Contract names package entitlements, environments, residency, support and exit.
5. Implementation activates kernel, identity and one lighthouse package.
6. Data migration is reconciled and signed.
7. UAT is persona and process based.
8. Go-live includes hypercare and adoption telemetry.
9. Quarterly value review recommends expansion based on outcomes.

## Public/privacy journey

External users receive a separate, mobile-ready Arabic-first portal:

- clear purpose and privacy notice;
- minimal identity verification proportional to request;
- save/resume where safe;
- opaque tracking reference;
- no internal IDs or tenant information;
- accessible status updates;
- safe upload;
- escalation/contact path.

## Journey governance

Every backlog item names its persona and journey step. A screen without a journey owner is a candidate for removal or administration-only placement.
