QUAL-CP-001 [Medium] CyberService is a God class: 613 LOC, 10 repositories, 8 unrelated entity domains in one service
FILE: tss-grc-backend/src/main/java/com/tss/grc/module/cyber/service/CyberService.java:23-613
RESOLUTION: Split CyberService into per-aggregate services (CyberControlService, CyberVulnerabilityService, CyberIncidentService, CyberAssetService, CyberEvidenceService, CyberActionPlanService, CyberAssessmentService) mirroring the asm/ and vuln_sla/ sub-package convention. Keep a thin CyberDashboardService for the cross-cutting getDashboard aggregation. The controller can fan out to the new services without API changes.

QUAL-CP-003 [Medium] PrivacyService swallows audit and event-bus exceptions with empty catch(Exception ignored) — no logging at all
FILE: tss-grc-backend/src/main/java/com/tss/grc/module/privacy/service/PrivacyService.java:157-185
RESOLUTION: Add @Slf4j to the class and log at WARN with the exception inside both catch blocks: `log.warn("SDAIA audit write failed for breach {} — continuing", id, ex);`. Catch a narrower type if feasible. Keep the non-fatal behaviour but make the failure observable.
