PERF-IO-003  [Medium]  In-thread Thread.sleep retry/backoff inside transactional integration calls extends DB-connection hold time to as much as ~75s-2min
FILE: tss-grc-backend/src/main/java/com/tss/grc/module/integrations/splunk/client/LiveSplunkClient.java LINES: 69-107
EFFORT: M
-- description --
Every LIVE client retries 5xx/connection failures with an in-thread exponential backoff that calls Thread.sleep on the calling thread. Splunk and CrowdStrike cap the per-attempt backoff at BACKOFF_CAP_MS = 30_000L (LiveSplunkClient.java:49, LiveCrowdStrikeClient.java:54) over up to 3 attempts. -- resolution --

Cap total retry budget (sum of timeouts + backoffs) well under the 60s leak-detection threshold and the 30s connection-timeout to avoid cascading symptoms.
