FILE: tss-grc-backend/src/main/resources/db/migration/sqlserver/V3__compliance_module.sql 15-108
RES : Adopt a schema-wide standard: every tenant table gets a nonclustered index with organisation_id as the leading key, extended with is_deleted (filtered `WHERE is_deleted = 0`) and the columns most queries sort/filter on (status, created_at). Backfill the 74 missing tables in a new Flyway migration so it applies in prod. For tables soft-deleted via @Where, use a filtered index to keep it small. Prioritise by row growth: risk.*, cyber.*, compliance.*, privacy.*, tprm.vendors, iam.users, platform.notifications.
