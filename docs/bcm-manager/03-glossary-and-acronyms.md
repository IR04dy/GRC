## Time-band conventions

| Band | Hours | Saudi context |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate | 0-1h | Crisis declaration window, SAMA major-incident initial assessment |
| Short | 1-6h | SAMA Cyber Framework v1.3 6h regulator-notification SLA |
| Medium | 6-24h | Critical-process recovery target |
| Long | 24-72h | NRC + SDAIA notification deadlines |
| Extended | 72h-7d | Strategic recovery + scaling back |
| Strategic | 7d+ | Programme restructure if disruption persists |

## Severity classification

| Severity | EN trigger | AR | SAMA reportable? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CRITICAL** | TIER_1 process unavailable > MTPD, regulatory breach, customer data exfiltration | حرجة | Yes — within 6h |
| **HIGH** | TIER_1 degraded > 50%, or TIER_2 unavailable > MTPD | عالية | Likely — assess within 2h |
| **MEDIUM** | TIER_2 degraded, TIER_3 unavailable > MTPD | متوسطة | No (unless escalates) |
| **LOW** | TIER_3 degraded, contained incident | منخفضة | No |
