# 12 · Pandemic / Public Health Plan

## 1 · Scope
Continuity during widespread health events — pandemic, epidemic, MOH-declared outbreak. Distinct from one-off illness which is HR-handled. Lessons from COVID-19 baked in.

## 2 · Trigger levels

| Level | Trigger | Posture |
|---|---|---|
| **L0 — Watch** | MOH or WHO advisory; regional outbreak | Monitor, prepare |
| **L1 — Heightened** | Confirmed cases in KSA; MOH guidance update | Limited measures (hand hygiene, sanitisation cadence) |
| **L2 — Restricted** | Sustained transmission; MOH/SCFHS instructions issued | Mandatory measures (masks, distancing, hybrid work) |
| **L3 — Lockdown** | Full or partial lockdown by Royal Decree | Full WFH enablement; site closures; emergency operations |

## 3 · Workforce continuity

### 3.1 Hybrid + WFH enablement
- 95% of office-based roles WFH-capable
- VPN concurrent capacity ≥ workforce
- Workstation deployment (laptop + headset) ≥ workforce
- Authentication: MFA + conditional access (already enforced via NCA ECC)

### 3.2 Branch + customer-facing continuity
- Reduced-hours protocol
- Skeleton-staffing roster (alternating teams)
- Drive-through + ATM-only operation as fallback
- Mobile banking + USSD *900# remains primary channel

### 3.3 Critical-skill protection
- TIER_1 roles split across geographic locations (Riyadh/Jeddah split for ops)
- Cross-training (no single-skilled L1)
- Succession plans validated

- Vendor surge: cloud burst, contract IT support, security operations augmentation
- Critical-vendor pandemic posture review (annual)
- Logistics: print/mail vendor coverage (statement printing)
- Power + utilities resilience verified

## 7 · Health & safety

- Daily symptom screening (if MOH mandates)
- Sanitation cadence
- PPE inventory
- Air-quality / filtration in offices
- Cooperation with MOH contact-tracing

## 8 · Mental health + welfare

- Employee assistance programme (EAP)
- Communication cadence to reduce uncertainty
- Manager training on remote-team mental-health checks
- Childcare / school-closure support

## 9 · Exercise

- Annual pandemic tabletop (mandatory)
- Periodic WFH surge test (capacity)
- VPN failover drill

## 10 · COVID-19 lessons captured

| Lesson | Action taken |
|---|---|
| WFH capacity insufficient | Expanded VPN + laptops to 100% workforce |
| Branch closures hurt elderly customers | Increased call-centre + video KYC capacity |
| Vendor concentration on consultancies | Diversified IT-services vendor pool |
| Family-care concerns slowed knowledge work | Manager flexibility + EAP funded |
| Mental health concerns post-isolation | EAP made permanent, expanded Arabic-language support |
